Help & support

How this prototype works

A short guide to the human-led project portfolio workflow.

01

Set the context

Save strategic objectives, budget, and staff capacity.

02

Propose and review

Enter project information, then record five manual ratings with a rationale for each.

03

Compare

Shortlist two to four evaluated projects and compare their profiles. There is no overall score or automatic ranking.

04

Build a scenario

Name a candidate portfolio, add shortlisted projects, and check budget and staff constraints.

05

Make a decision

Use evidence, trade-offs, and feasibility warnings to support a final human decision.
